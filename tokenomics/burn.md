# 🔥 WLM Token Burn Mechanism

Walme's burn mechanism is a key part of WLM tokenomics, designed to preserve the token’s long-term value by reducing its circulating supply and increasing scarcity.

---

## 💡 Why Token Burning?

Token burning is the process of permanently removing tokens from circulation. This helps:
- Reduce inflationary pressure
- Increase token scarcity
- Enhance long-term value
- Encourage long-term holding and ecosystem participation

---

## 🧩 How It Works

WLM supports **two types of burns**:

### 1. **Periodic Buyback & Burn**
Walme allocates a share of its revenue (from fees, services, premium subscriptions, etc.) to buy WLM tokens on the open market and burn them on-chain.

### 2. **User-Initiated Burn**
Users can choose to burn their WLM tokens directly from their wallet, often as part of:
- Premium access to exclusive services
- DAO-level upgrades
- Staking multipliers or early unlocks
- Governance reputation boosting

---

## 🔄 Burning Process

- **Network**: Solana (SPL), with bridges to Ethereum and Polygon  
- **Smart Contract**: Tokens are sent to an irretrievable burn address  
- **Explorer**: All burns are tracked publicly and verifiably on-chain

Example burn address (Solana):

`4BurnxxburnxxxWLMxxxyyyyzzzdead111111`
